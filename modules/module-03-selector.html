Protection & Heat Management

Shield hose assemblies from harsh conditions

Abrasion, heat, and environmental exposure are leading contributors to early hose failure. Protection accessories wrap around the assembly to absorb damage before it reaches the hose itself — extending service life and reducing unplanned downtime.

🔥 Fire & Thermal Sleeves

Protect against high-temperature environments and radiant heat exposure.

🛡️ Abrasion Sleeves

Guard against surface wear from contact with machinery, edges, or other hoses.

🌀 Wire Spring Guards

Provide flexible mechanical protection while maintaining hose movement.

🔄 Plastic Spiral Guards

Wrap hoses for protection in high-movement or routing-intensive setups.

Best Used In High-temperature environments, applications with constant hose movement, equipment with tight routing near sharp edges or other surfaces.
Bend Control & Strain Reduction

Maintain proper routing and reduce connection stress

Excessive bending near fittings and connection points is a common cause of premature hose failure. Bend control accessories limit the angle at which a hose can flex, distributing stress more evenly and reducing fatigue at the most vulnerable points in the assembly.

↩️ Bend Restrictors

Fit over the hose at connection points to limit bending radius and reduce strain where the hose meets the fitting — one of the highest-stress areas in any hose assembly.

Best Used In Dynamic applications with repeated hose movement, assemblies routed around corners, and anywhere connection points experience repeated flex or vibration.
Connections & Flow Control

Ensure proper system integration and flexibility

A hose assembly is only as reliable as the connections holding it in place. Connection accessories allow assemblies to integrate cleanly with existing system ports, fittings, and equipment — and make assembly changes faster and more consistent.

🔧 Hydraulic Adapters

Allow hose assemblies to connect across different port types, thread standards, and fitting configurations.

⚡ Quick Connects

Enable fast, tool-free connections for hose assemblies that need to be swapped or serviced frequently.

Best Used In Systems with multiple port configurations, operations that require frequent assembly changeovers, and applications where connection consistency is critical.
Handling & Safety

Support safe operation and hose management

In active work environments, how a hose is handled, stored, and managed has a direct impact on both operator safety and hose longevity. Handling accessories help control positioning, reduce trip hazards, and keep assemblies organized and accessible.

🦺 Operator Safety Systems

Designed to protect operators from hose whip, unexpected pressure release, or contact with moving assemblies.

🔁 Retractable Reels

Keep hoses organized, off the floor, and within reach — reducing wear from dragging and improving overall work area safety.

Best Used In High-traffic work areas, overhead or mobile hose drops, applications with high-pressure assemblies where operator exposure is a concern.

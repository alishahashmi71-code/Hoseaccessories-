Built for Real Operating Conditions

In many applications, hose failure doesn't start with the hose. It starts with abrasion, heat, strain, or improper connections. Accessories help address those risks.

Accessories help protect your hose assembly, connect it to the system, and keep it performing in real-world conditions — reducing wear, managing strain, and supporting safe operation.

Accessories help address these risks by:

  • Reducing wear from abrasion and movement
  • Managing bend radius and strain
  • Protecting against heat and environmental exposure
  • Supporting safe and secure connections

Used correctly, they help extend service life and simplify maintenance.
